tmDataQualityAnalyzer v2.14.1 - What's New
=================================

  A corrective release. Two things v2.14.0 got wrong are fixed, one control is
  removed, and the user manual's screenshots are brought up to date. Nothing
  about how streams are processed, calibrated or exported has changed.

  Fixed:

  - Time labels now land on round clock times. v2.14.0 made the axis step in
    intervals you would name - 15 seconds, 5 minutes - but it measured those
    intervals from the start of the recording rather than from the clock. A file
    that began at 10:30:17 produced labels reading :17, :47, :17 - correctly
    spaced thirty seconds apart, and never on a round second.

    Recordings that happened to begin exactly on a round second looked right,
    which is why frame sync files usually did and receiver SNR files usually did
    not. The axis now places its labels on round clock times whatever time the
    recording started.

  - Dialogs follow the light theme. The processing dialog, and every other
    dialog, kept a dark title bar after switching to the light theme. The main
    window was unaffected because it draws its own title bar, which is why this
    went unnoticed.

  Changed:

  - The Toggle Legend button has been removed from the chart. Showing and hiding
    the legend is still available from the right-click menu (Show Legend) and
    from the L key, which is where it has been since those were added - the
    button was a third way to do the same thing, and it occupied chart space
    permanently to do it.

  - The user manual's screenshots are re-captured. Thirteen figures were showing
    an older version of the window, including one that had been out of date for
    four releases.

  Under the hood:

  - Built against Qt 6.12.0.
