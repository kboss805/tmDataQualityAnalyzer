tmDataQualityAnalyzer v2.14.0 - What's New
=================================

  Four improvements to the plot window, all of them about reading the data
  rather than processing it. Nothing about how streams are processed, calibrated
  or exported has changed.

  New:

  - Zoom Back. Right-click the plot and use X Axis > Zoom Back, or press
    Backspace, to return to the view you were looking at before the last zoom -
    one level at a time. Drilling into a loss-of-lock and retracing your way out
    no longer means starting again from the full recording.

    It remembers the zooms you chose deliberately - a drag across the plot, or a
    time window you typed - and ignores the mouse wheel and panning, so the list
    stays the handful of views you meant rather than every notch of the wheel.
    Reset Span still returns the whole recording and forgets the levels.

  Improved:

  - A dense trace now shows how much the signal moved, not just where it
    averaged. Where a recording has more samples than the plot has pixels, each
    column is drawn as a band from its lowest to its highest value, with the line
    running through the column's average. Previously those columns were drawn as
    a single outline, which on a noisy receiver read as one solid shape with no
    indication of where inside it the signal actually sat.

    A one-sample dropout is still drawn as sharply as before - it is the bottom
    of its column, and the band's edge is drawn solid for exactly that reason.

  - Time labels land on round times. The axis used to divide whatever span was on
    screen into equal parts, so a two-minute view produced labels 29 seconds
    apart. It now steps in intervals you would actually name - 15 seconds, 5
    minutes, 6 hours - and places them on round times within the view.

  - The plot redraws instantly. Moving the mouse across the chart, dragging the
    legend or showing a tooltip used to redraw every curve from scratch; on a
    large recording that was most of a second each time, which is what made the
    window feel sluggish. The drawn plot is now kept and reused until something
    about it actually changes.

    Together with the previous release's work, a plot of twelve channels over an
    hour at 10 ms resolution has gone from roughly a minute and a half per redraw
    to under a thousandth of a second.
